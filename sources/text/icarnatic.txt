iCarnatic, NFP (Not for Profit Organization based in USA)
Welcome to iCarnatic.org
iCarnatic is a not-for-profit organization formed for promoting Indian classical music, dance and arts through events, concerts, webcasts and educational support.
iCarnatic has been approved as a Section 501(c)(3) organization. Donations to iCarnatic are tax deductible under US Federal Income Tax.

iCarnatic Summer Music & Dance Festival 2026 (Aug 22 & 23, 2026) with Chicago Tyagaraja Utsavam
iCarnatic Summer Music and Dance Festival is scheduled on August 22 & 23, 2026. This year we are partnering with Chicago Tyagaraja Utsavam as part of their 50th year celebrations.
We received an overwhelming number of applications this year. We thank all the artists for their interest in participating in the festival.
VENUE: Sai Samsthan USA, 1101 Foran Ln, Aurora, IL 60506
Live Webcast: Please visit iCarnatic YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@icarnatic for live webcast. This is subject to internet availability and may be interrupted due to technical issues. We apologize for any inconvenience.

iCarnatic Margazhi in Detroit 2026 (November 26–28, 2026) - Tribute to Sri T.R. Mahalingam (Mali)
This year, the MID festival is proud to honor the enduring legacy of the legendary flautist, Sri T.R. Mahalingam (Mali). We invite you to join us for this special tribute, held over the US Thanksgiving holiday weekend (November 26–28). Please note that the schedule may expand to include an opening session on the evening of the 25th and closing performances through noon on the 29th. If you are interested in contributing to this celebration, we welcome you to submit your participation application via the form below (Application window closes on 15th of July).
