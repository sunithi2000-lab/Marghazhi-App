"Programs for the month of July-2025"
Ragam-Tanam-Pallavi (RTP)Annual Series 2025 - Tami Isai Saaral Monthly program, Music Concerts, Discourses, Dance and Drama.
A special curation of Ragam-Tanam-Pallavi (RTP) recitals-a celebration of the most profound and creative expression in Carnatic music. This exclusive series will feature eminent vidwans, vidushis and Next-gen artists, each offering a unique interpretation of RTP, exploring both traditional grandeur and contemporary innovation.
What to Expect:
Handpicked ragas, rarely heard pallavis, and intricate rhythmic explorations
Performances by renowned artists known for their RTP mastery
Rasika Ranjani Sabha's initiative to encourage young artists to perform RTP in the earlier slot
Occasional Ragamalika, Trikalam, and Tanam on instruments like veena, violin, and flute
Curated commentary and insights on each RTP for a deeper appreciation
