About the Event
The Music Academy's landmark centenary edition of its annual December music season, featuring Carnatic vocal and instrumental concerts across daily slots, with morning concerts free and evening concerts ticketed.Line-up published so far: 4 days, 16 December 2026 to 19 December 2026, at the T.T.K. Auditorium, No. 168, T.T.K. Road, Royapettah, Chennai 600014. The season itself runs to 1 January 2027; the remaining days will be added as the Academy announces them.Morning and afternoon concerts are free; the 4:00 PM and 6:45 PM concerts are ticketed.
