Sanjay Subrahmanyan
Renowned Carnatic Vocalist | Padma Shri Awardee | Acclaimed Musician & Composer
Sanjay Subrahmanyan is one of India's foremost Carnatic vocalists, celebrated for his exceptional artistry, scholarly approach, and innovative concert presentations. He is admired for his mastery of ragas, manodharma, and classical compositions. He has performed at leading music festivals and sabhas across the world, inspiring audiences with his powerful voice and rich musical heritage.
